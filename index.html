About Basel Mail

            

              Basel Mail is a productivity tool for preparing, personalizing,
              organizing, and reusing email content. The production version uses
              local browser storage only and does not use a Basel Mail backend
              service.
            


            
Local storage

            

              Templates, drafts, settings, and queued emails are stored in this
              browser on this device using browser local storage. Basel Mail
              does not upload that workspace data to a Basel Mail backend.
            

            

              Local data may be lost if browser or site data is cleared, private
              browsing is used, the browser or device is changed, or storage
              becomes unavailable. Export templates if you need a separate
              backup.
            


            
Email handoff

            

              Basel Mail only prepares email content. When you choose to open a
              message in Outlook or your default email application, the
              recipient, subject, and message body are handed to that email
              application for your review. Basel Mail does not automatically
              send emails.
            


            
Security and responsibilities

            

              Browser local storage is not application-level encrypted. Use
              Basel Mail only for information you are authorized to process and
              follow your organization's data-protection, confidentiality,
              security, and records-management requirements.
            

            

              Always review recipients, subject lines, message content, links,
              personalization, and accuracy before sending.
